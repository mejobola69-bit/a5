The Evolution of Urban Weatherproof Jacket Design

By Outerwear Architect • 7 Min Read

Modern cities demand outerwear that seamlessly transitions from rainy commuter streets to refined boardroom meetings without looking like heavy mountaineering gear.

1. Concealed Technical Waterproofing

Modern jacket design integrates microporous 20,000mm membranes behind matte wool blends and silent nylon shells, concealing stormproof tech within clean silhouette lines.

2. Ergonomic Articulated Sleeves

3D articulated sleeve patterning and gusseted underarms allow full arm motion for cycling and urban transit without pulling the hem upward.
