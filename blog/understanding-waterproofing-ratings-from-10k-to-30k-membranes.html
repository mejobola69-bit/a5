Understanding Waterproofing Ratings: From 10K to 30K Membranes

By Textile Scientist • 8 Min Read

Outerwear performance labels feature ratings like **20,000mm / 20,000g**. Understanding hydrostatic head tests helps buyers choose appropriate storm protection.

1. Hydrostatic Head Water Pressure (10K vs 20K vs 30K)

A **20,000mm rating** means a 1-inch square column of water can stand 20,000 millimeters tall on the fabric before liquid penetrates, representing torrential downpour defense.

2. Breathability & Moisture Vapor Transport (g/m²/24hr)

High breathability ratings (20,000g+) allow internal body perspiration vapor to escape through microscopic pores without letting rain droplets inside.
