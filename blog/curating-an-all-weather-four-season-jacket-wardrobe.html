Curating an All-Weather Four-Season Jacket Wardrobe

By Wardrobe Architect • 7 Min Read

Building a capsule outerwear wardrobe requires only 3 versatile jacket archetypes engineered to handle every weather forecast throughout the year.

1. The 3-Pillar Outerwear Formula

Combine: 1) A 20K Waterproof Technical Shell for spring rains, 2) A Tailored Wool Blazer for autumn business casual, and 3) An 800-Fill Arctic Down Parka for freezing winter storms.

2. Modular Zip-In System Compatibility

Selecting shell jackets compatible with zip-in fleece or liner jackets turns your 3 garments into 6 climate configurations.
