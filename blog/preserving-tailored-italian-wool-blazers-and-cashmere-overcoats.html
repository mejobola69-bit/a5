Preserving Tailored Italian Wool Blazers and Cashmere Overcoats

By Master Tailor • 8 Min Read

Luxury tailoring crafted from virgin Italian wool requires mindful garment care to retain its drape, shoulder structure, and natural moisture balance.

1. Wide Contoured Wooden Hangers

Always support structured wool blazers on wide mahogany or cedar hangers that match natural shoulder width to prevent collar deformation and seam sag.

2. Natural Horsehair Brushing & Steaming

Brush wool coats after every wear with a soft horsehair clothes brush to dislodge street dust. Use vertical steam instead of dry cleaning to refresh fibers.
